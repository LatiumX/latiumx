How it works

Four steps, one escrow program

  1. 1 · Poster

    Fund the task

    Describe the work, set a deadline and deposit LTMX. The brief is stored on IPFS and its hash goes on-chain.

  2. 2 · Worker

    Claim it

    One worker claims the task. Other workers see it as taken so nobody does the job twice.

  3. 3 · Worker

    Submit proof

    Deliver the work and submit a link or file hash as proof.

  4. 4 · Escrow

    Release payment

    The poster approves and the escrow pays out. If the poster goes silent past the review window, the worker can release it. Disagreements go to a reviewer multisig.

LTMX launches through pump.fun. The escrow program (written for Solana with Anchor) and the task board come next, and will run on devnet before anything handles real funds.

LTMX is the unit tasks are priced and paid in. It launches as a fair launch on pump.fun: the whole supply starts on pump.fun's bonding curve and everyone, the team included, buys through the same curve. There is no presale, no private round and no pre-minted team or treasury wallet.
